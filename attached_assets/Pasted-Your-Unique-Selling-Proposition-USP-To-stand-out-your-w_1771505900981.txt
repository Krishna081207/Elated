Your Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
To stand out, your website shouldn't just list menus. It should answer the question: "What does it feel like to sit there?"

Service & Layout: Show "Work-from-cafe" readiness (Wi-Fi speed, power sockets), "Date night" corners, or "Pet-friendly" zones.

Visual-First: Instead of food photos, use 360° views or short videos of the interior.

Real-time Vibe: A "Crowd Meter" (e.g., Quiet, Bustling, Full) or "Music Genre" tag for the day.

Small/Hidden Gems: Specifically target "off-beat" spots that aren't on major apps because they don't want to pay the high 20-30% delivery commissions.
